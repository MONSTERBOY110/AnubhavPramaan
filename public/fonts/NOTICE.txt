AnubhavPramaan - vendored web fonts
===================================

Self-hosted rather than loaded from a CDN, so the installed app renders Hindi with the
network off (offline assessment camps). Both faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1, whose full text is in OFL-1.1.txt beside this file.
